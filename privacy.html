Privacy Policy

This website respects user privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users when accessing this website and related services.

This may include information provided during account registration, communication with support, verification processes or use of platform features.

Information provided directly by users may include a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, account information, transaction-related information or other details reasonably required to provide the relevant service.

Technical information may also be collected automatically, including IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, language preferences and information about how users interact with the website.

Information may be used for legitimate operational and service-related purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing and maintaining website services.
  • Processing and managing transactions where relevant.

  • Responding to enquiries and providing support.
  • Protecting accounts, systems and users against unauthorized activity.
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent or suspicious activity.
  • Improving website functionality and usability.
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
  • Sending important service-related communications.

Anonymous or aggregated information may also be used for analytics, research and service improvement where permitted.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website operation, remember user preferences, understand how visitors interact with pages and improve performance.

Depending on the user's browser and location, certain cookies may be controlled or disabled through browser settings or available preference tools.

Disabling some cookies may affect the availability or performance of certain website features.

Personal information is not treated as publicly available information simply because a user accesses this website.

Personal information should generally be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
