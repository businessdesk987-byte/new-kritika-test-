Complaints & Dispute Resolution

This website aims to provide a clear, accurate and transparent gaming experience. If you believe there has been an issue involving our Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, website content, account-related matters, transactions, promotions or another concern connected with the website, you may submit a complaint so the matter can be reviewed appropriately.

1. Raising a Complaint

Users may raise a complaint through the available contact or support channels provided on this website.

When submitting a complaint, users are encouraged to provide enough information for the issue to be properly understood and reviewed.

Relevant information may include account details where appropriate, transaction information, dates, screenshots, messages or a clear description of the concern.

Providing accurate and complete information can help make the review process more efficient.

2. Complaint Review

After receiving a complaint, the available information and the circumstances of the issue may be reviewed.

Additional information, clarification or verification may be requested where reasonably necessary to understand the complaint.

Depending on the nature of the issue, relevant account information, transactions, platform activity, communications or other available records may be reviewed.

Users are encouraged to cooperate reasonably if additional information is requested during the review process.

3. Response to Complaints

Complaints should be reviewed and responded to within a reasonable period.

The amount of time required may vary depending on the complexity of the issue, the information available and whether additional verification or investigation is required.

If additional time is needed, the user may be informed that the review is still ongoing.

A response may explain the outcome of the review, any action taken and any further steps that may be available.

4. Disputes

If a user disagrees with the outcome of a complaint, they may request further review where such a process is available.

Users should clearly explain why they disagree with the original response and provide any additional information or evidence they believe should be considered.

The additional information may then be reviewed and a further response may be provided where appropriate.

5. Account and Transaction Concerns

Additional verification may be required when a complaint involves account access, identity verification, transactions, promotions or other account-related matters.

For security reasons, the relevant service provider may need to confirm the identity of the account holder before providing account-specific information or making changes to an account.

Suspected unauthorized use, account misuse or suspicious activity should be reported as soon as possible so the matter can be reviewed.

6. External Resolution

After the applicable internal complaint process has been completed, users may have additional rights or remedies under the laws or regulations that apply in their location.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include access to an independent complaint-resolution process, regulator, authority or other appropriate forum.

Available external resolution options may vary depending on the user's location, the nature of the complaint and the service involved.

7. Fair and Respectful Communication

Users and support representatives are encouraged to communicate respectfully throughout the complaint process.

Complaints should be expressed clearly and honestly. Abusive, threatening, deliberately misleading or inappropriate communications may affect how the matter is handled, subject to applicable requirements.

Complaints should be assessed on the basis of the available information, evidence and circumstances relevant to the issue.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Complaints & Dispute Resolution Policy may be updated when website services, procedures or applicable requirements change.

The latest version published on this website will apply from the date stated in the updated policy.

If you have a complaint or dispute, use the official contact or support channels available on this website and provide complete and accurate information where possible.
