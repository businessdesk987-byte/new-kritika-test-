18+ Age Policy

This website is intended only for users who meet the applicable minimum age requirements. In most cases, access is intended for users who are at least 18 years old, although a higher minimum age may apply depending on the laws or regulations in the user's location.

Age Requirement

By accessing or using this website, you confirm that you meet the applicable minimum age requirement. Users who are under the required age should not create an account, access restricted areas or use age-restricted services.

Age restrictions are intended to help ensure that adult-only features are used only by people who are legally permitted to access them. Users are responsible for understanding and meeting the age requirements that apply in their location.

Age Verification

Where appropriate, this website or the relevant service provider may use age-verification measures to confirm that a user satisfies the applicable minimum age requirement.

In some cases, users may be asked to provide accurate information or supporting documents during registration or at a later stage.

Information provided for verification purposes must be accurate and up to date. Users should not provide false information or use another person's details in an attempt to satisfy age-verification requirements.

Where additional verification is required, access to certain account features may remain restricted until the verification process has been completed.

Accurate Registration Information

When creating an account, users should provide accurate information about themselves, including their correct date of birth and any other information requested during registration.

Users should not create accounts on behalf of other people or allow another person to use their own account.

Providing false information in order to bypass an age requirement may result in the account being reviewed, restricted or blocked by the relevant service provider.

Underage Access

This website is not intended for users who are below the applicable minimum age.

If it becomes known that an underage person has accessed an age-restricted area or created an account where they are not permitted to do so, appropriate action may be taken by the relevant service provider, including restricting or removing access.

Parents, guardians or other responsible adults who become aware of underage access should contact the appropriate support team or service provider so the matter can be reviewed.

Parents and guardians are also encouraged to use appropriate parental controls and supervision to help prevent minors from accessing age-restricted online services.

Protecting Access to Your Account

Users are responsible for keeping login credentials secure and confidential.

Account credentials should not be shared with minors or with anyone who is not permitted to use the account.

If unauthorized access or misuse of account credentials is suspected, users should contact the relevant support team or platform provider as soon as possible.

Location-Specific Requirements

Minimum age requirements can vary between countries, regions and jurisdictions. Some locations may require users to be older than 18 or to satisfy additional conditions before accessing certain online gaming features or services.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of any service complies with the laws and regulations that apply in their location.

Where access is restricted by law or by the service provider, users should not attempt to bypass those restrictions.

Our Commitment

This website supports responsible access to age-restricted gaming information and services.

Where account-based services are provided by a third party, that provider may review or verify user information if there is a reasonable concern about age or registration details.

By accessing age-restricted services, users confirm that they satisfy the applicable minimum age requirements and will provide truthful information where verification is required.

Users who are below the applicable minimum age should not access or use age-restricted services.
