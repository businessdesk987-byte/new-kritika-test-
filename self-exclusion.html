Self-Exclusion Policy

This website supports responsible gaming and recognizes that some users may need to take a temporary or longer break from gaming activity. Self-exclusion is intended to help users restrict their own access when they feel that stepping away would be beneficial.

Requesting Self-Exclusion

Where self-exclusion features are available, users may request temporary or longer-term restrictions through the relevant account settings or by contacting the appropriate support team.

Users may be asked to choose an exclusion period and confirm their request before the restriction is applied.

Depending on the selected period and the rules of the relevant platform, the restriction may remain active for a fixed amount of time or until specific conditions have been satisfied.

During the Exclusion Period

Once self-exclusion has been activated, access to certain account features or gaming services may be restricted for the duration of the selected period.

Users should not attempt to avoid an active self-exclusion by creating another account, using another person's account or finding other ways to bypass the restriction.

The relevant service provider may use reasonable measures to identify attempts to avoid an active self-exclusion. Such activity may result in additional account restrictions where permitted by the applicable terms.

Choosing the Right Period

Users should carefully consider the length of a self-exclusion period before confirming the request.

Where a minimum exclusion period applies, it may not be possible to remove the restriction before that period has ended.

Self-exclusion should therefore be treated as a deliberate account-control measure rather than a short-term setting that can always be reversed immediately.

Account and Access Restrictions

During an active self-exclusion period, access to the relevant account and associated gaming features may be limited or blocked.

Some account functions may remain unavailable until the exclusion period has ended or until any required review has been completed.

Users should not share their account credentials with another person or ask someone else to access the account on their behalf.

New Account Restrictions

Self-exclusion applies to the individual who requested the restriction and is intended to prevent access to the relevant gaming services during the selected period.

Users should not create additional accounts for the purpose of avoiding an existing self-exclusion.

If a service provider identifies a new account that appears to have been created to bypass an active restriction, the account may be reviewed and further limitations may be applied.

Reactivation After Exclusion

Access may become available again, where permitted, after the selected self-exclusion period has ended.

The end of an exclusion period does not always mean that an account will be reactivated automatically.

The relevant service provider may require additional confirmation, account review or verification before restoring access.

Responsible Account Management

Self-exclusion is one of several tools that may help users manage their gaming activity.

Other options may include taking temporary breaks, setting personal limits, using session reminders or reviewing account activity.

Users who feel that they need a longer break should review the account-control options available through the relevant gaming service or contact its support team for further information.

Support

Users who have questions about self-exclusion, account restrictions or available control tools should contact the relevant gaming platform or service provider.

Support teams may be able to explain the available options, the duration of restrictions and any steps required to activate or manage a self-exclusion request.

Users are encouraged to take responsibility for their account access and make use of available self-exclusion or account-control tools whenever they feel those tools may be helpful.
