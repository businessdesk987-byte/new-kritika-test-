Problem Gaming Help

This website encourages users to remain aware of their gaming behaviour and to seek support if gaming begins to feel difficult to control. Problem gaming can affect finances, daily routines, relationships, work, studies, sleep and overall wellbeing.

Recognising possible concerns early and taking appropriate action may help users regain control over their gaming activity.

Recognizing Potential Concerns

Every person’s situation is different, and there is no single sign that proves a problem exists. However, changes in normal behaviour may be a reason to review gaming activity more carefully.

Possible warning signs may include spending more time or money than originally planned, trying to recover previous losses, finding it difficult to stop, hiding gaming activity from family or friends, or allowing gaming to interfere with work, study, sleep or other responsibilities.

Users who notice similar patterns may wish to take a break and review the account-control options available through the relevant gaming platform.

Take a Step Back

If gaming begins to feel difficult to manage, stepping away from the platform can be a helpful action.

Users may consider temporary breaks, time-management tools, personal limits or self-exclusion options where these features are available.

Setting limits before beginning a gaming session and respecting those limits can help users maintain greater control over their activity.

Increasing spending or extending gaming sessions because of previous results may make it more difficult to stay within personal limits.

Taking time away from gaming can also provide an opportunity to consider how gaming is affecting other areas of daily life.

Financial Awareness

Users should only spend amounts they are comfortable with and should always prioritise essential expenses such as housing, bills, food, education, debt repayments and other financial responsibilities.

Borrowed money or funds intended for essential needs should not be used for gaming activity.

Financial decisions should not be based on the expectation of recovering previous losses.

If gaming is creating financial stress, users should consider stopping their gaming activity and seeking advice from an appropriate financial professional or support organisation.

Talk to Someone

Speaking with someone can be helpful when gaming activity begins to feel difficult to manage.

This may include a family member, trusted friend, counsellor, financial professional or another suitably qualified support person.

Users do not have to deal with concerns on their own. Discussing changes in spending, behaviour or online activity may help identify practical steps for regaining control.

Use Available Account Controls

Gaming platforms may provide tools that help users manage their account activity.

These may include spending limits, session controls, temporary account restrictions, cooling-off periods, reminders or self-exclusion features where available.

Users who feel they need a longer break may consider self-exclusion or other account-management tools offered by the relevant platform.

Once a restriction has been activated, users should not attempt to bypass it by creating or using another account.

Getting Professional Support

If gaming is affecting finances, relationships, work, studies, sleep or other areas of daily life, professional support may be appropriate.

Qualified counsellors, mental health professionals, financial advisers or other relevant support organisations may be able to provide assistance based on the individual's circumstances.

This website can provide general information about gaming awareness and account-control options, but it does not replace professional medical, psychological or financial advice.

Our Approach

This website aims to provide general information that helps users better understand and manage their gaming activity.

Users are encouraged to monitor their behaviour, set reasonable personal limits, take breaks when needed and make use of available account-control tools.

If a user feels that gaming has become difficult to control, taking action as early as possible may be helpful.

Users should review the support and restriction options provided by the relevant gaming platform and seek additional professional help where appropriate.
