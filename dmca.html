DMCA / Copyright Policy

This website respects intellectual property rights and expects users, contributors and third-party content providers to do the same. This DMCA / Copyright Policy explains how copyright concerns may be reported and how valid notices relating to content available on this website may be reviewed.

1. Respect for Copyright

Copyright, trademark and other intellectual property protections may apply to content available on this website, including text, images, graphics, logos, videos, audio, software, designs and other materials.

Users should not reproduce, copy, distribute, modify, publish or otherwise use protected material without appropriate permission from the rights holder or another lawful basis.

2. Copyright Infringement Notice

If you believe that copyrighted material owned by you has been used or made available on this website without appropriate permission, you may submit a written copyright complaint.

To help us review the matter, a copyright notice should generally include:

  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • A description of the material you believe is infringing.
  • Information that allows us to locate the relevant material on the website.
  • Your name and contact information.
  • A statement confirming your good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or applicable law.
  • A statement confirming that the information provided in the notice is accurate and, where applicable, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Providing complete and accurate information can help make the review process more efficient.

3. Review of Complaints

After receiving a copyright complaint, the information provided may be reviewed and appropriate action may be taken where necessary.

Depending on the circumstances, the material identified in a valid complaint may be removed, restricted, disabled or otherwise addressed.

Additional information may also be requested from the person submitting the complaint or from the relevant content provider.

Submission of a copyright notice does not automatically establish that infringement has occurred. The notice, available information, applicable law and surrounding circumstances may all be considered before action is taken.

4. Counter-Notification

If content is removed or restricted following a copyright complaint and the affected party believes the action was taken in error, they may be able to submit a counter-notification where permitted by applicable law.

A counter-notification should explain why the removal or restriction is believed to be incorrect and should include any information reasonably necessary to review the matter.

Counter-notifications may be reviewed in accordance with applicable copyright requirements.

5. Repeat Infringement

Where appropriate, additional action may be taken against users or accounts that repeatedly provide or publish content that infringes intellectual property rights.

Depending on the circumstances, such action may include content restrictions, account limitations, suspension or termination.

6. False or Misleading Notices

Copyright complaints should only be submitted where there is a legitimate basis for the claim.

Providing false, incomplete or deliberately misleading information in a copyright notice may have legal consequences.

Where there is evidence that the copyright-reporting process has been abused, appropriate action may be taken.

7. Third-Party Content

This website may contain content, links or materials provided by third parties.

Such content may be subject to copyright, licensing or other permission requirements that remain the responsibility of the relevant content provider or rights holder.

If you believe third-party material appearing on this website infringes your copyright, you may submit a notice containing the information described above.

8. Contact

Questions or complaints relating to copyright matters should be submitted through the copyright, legal or contact channel made available on this website.

Rights holders are encouraged to provide enough information to identify both the copyrighted work and the material believed to be infringing so that the matter can be reviewed efficiently.

This policy may be updated from time to time as website practices, services and applicable copyright requirements change.
