Cookie Policy

Cookies and similar technologies may be used on this website to support functionality, understand how visitors use the site and improve the overall user experience. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, why they may be used and how users can manage their preferences.

By continuing to use this website, cookies may be used in accordance with this policy. Depending on the user's location and the technologies used, certain pages may provide options to accept, reject or manage non-essential cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a user's device when the website is visited.

Cookies can help a website recognize a browser or device, remember information from previous visits and support certain website functions.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as website functionality, navigation, remembering preferences, personalization, security and measuring website performance.

Cookies may be temporary or persistent. Session cookies are generally removed when the browser is closed, while persistent cookies may remain on the device for a defined period or until the user deletes them.

2. How This Website Uses Cookies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Supporting essential website functionality.
  • Maintaining login sessions where relevant.
  • Remembering selected settings and preferences.
  • Improving website usability and navigation.
  • Understanding general website usage.
  • Measuring website performance.
  • Identifying technical problems.
  • Supporting security and fraud-prevention measures.
  • Providing relevant website information where permitted.

The specific cookies used may vary depending on the features available on the website and how users interact with those features.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are used to support functions that may be necessary for the website to operate properly.

These may include login functionality, security controls, session management and basic navigation.

Some essential cookies may not be possible to disable through website preference tools because doing so could prevent certain features from working correctly.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may store information about user preferences or website settings to provide a more convenient experience when a visitor returns.

These cookies may help remember selected options so users do not need to configure the same settings repeatedly.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies may provide information about how visitors use the website.

This may include information about page visits, session activity, browser types, device categories and general interaction patterns.

Analytics information may be aggregated and used to identify technical issues, understand website performance and improve usability.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may remember settings selected during previous visits.

Their purpose is to provide a more consistent experience and reduce the need for users to repeatedly select the same preferences.

Security Cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be used to help protect the website and its users against unauthorized, abusive, fraudulent or suspicious activity.

These technologies may support account security, request monitoring and other security-related functions.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party service providers that support website functionality.

Third-party providers may supply services such as analytics, security, technical support, content delivery or other operational features.

These providers may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies.

Users are encouraged to review the relevant policies of third-party services where appropriate.

5. Managing Cookie Preferences

Where available, this website may provide a cookie banner or preference-management tool that allows users to manage non-essential cookies.

Cookies can also usually be controlled through browser settings.

Most modern browsers allow users to view stored cookies, delete cookies, block certain cookies or prevent websites from storing cookies in the future.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality or prevent some features from operating as intended.

6. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, this website may use similar technologies such as pixels, tags, local storage or other browser-based technologies where appropriate.

These technologies may be used for purposes similar to cookies, including remembering preferences, counting visits, measuring performance or supporting security.

Where required, their use will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, technology, service providers or applicable legal requirements.

Where changes are made, an updated version of this policy and, where appropriate, a revised effective date may be published on the website.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for the latest information.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about the use of cookies or similar technologies on this website, you may use the available contact channels provided on the site.

The purpose of this policy is to provide users with clear information about the technologies that may be used and, where required by law, appropriate options for managing non-essential cookies.
